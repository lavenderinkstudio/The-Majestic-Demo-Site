(Questions)

Asked often

Can buyers living abroad purchase through you?

Yes. Non-resident Indians and OCI cardholders can buy homes in India, though not agricultural land or plantations. We work with lawyers who handle the paperwork, and we can run a first viewing by video before you fly in.

How does a private viewing work?

We agree a time with you, and your adviser meets you at the house. Take as long as you like; most viewings fill an afternoon. Nobody else will be there.

Why aren't all your homes listed here?

Many owners prefer privacy. The eight residences on this site belong to sellers who are happy to be seen. Your adviser can tell you about others in confidence.

What does the online valuation tell me?

An indicative range, from local rates adjusted for the house itself. It can't see the light, the neighbours or the title deeds. For a figure to rely on, an adviser will visit.

Do you handle due diligence?

We arrange title searches, RERA checks for newer buildings and a structural survey for older homes, all through independent firms. You receive every report in full.

What do you charge?

Buyers pay us nothing. Sellers pay an agreed fee on completion, set out in writing before we begin.
