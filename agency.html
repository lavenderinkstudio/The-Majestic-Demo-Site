(The Agency)

A small house, by design

Three advisers, four offices and a few houses at a time. We would rather know eight homes completely than eighty in passing.

(Our story)

Begun on a verandah in 2008

The Majestic began when a family in Alibaug asked a friend to find the right buyer for their grandmother's house: someone who would keep the mango trees and the old basalt steps. It took a year, and the buyer still sends photographs every monsoon.

We have worked the same way since. We take on a few homes at a time, usually ones with a story, and learn everything about them: the title history, the drainage, which rooms catch the morning light and what the neighbours are like.

Buyers come to us through introductions, and increasingly through the concierge on this site. Either way, they meet an adviser who knows the house as well as its owners do.
